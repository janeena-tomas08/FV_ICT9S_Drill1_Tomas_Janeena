🏸 Beginner's Guide to Badminton

📋 Basic Rules

  • Matches are played to 21 points.
  • Rally scoring system applies.
  • Serves must be struck underhand.
  • Shuttlecock must land inside court lines.

🏸 Equipment

  • Badminton Racket
  • Shuttlecock
  • Badminton Net
  • Rubber Shoes/Sports Shoes
